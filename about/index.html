About

Environment shapes behavior. The spaces we live, work, and create in influence performance, clarity, relationships, and identity.

I'm Christine — a connector and strategist who brings together the people, places, and opportunities that help ideas move forward.

My work connects hospitality, real estate, community building, and creative entrepreneurship. Across each of those worlds, I keep returning to the same question: what environment will help this person, project, or conversation do its best work?

I help founders find clarity, connect brands with thoughtful partners, and design experiences where useful conversations can happen. I also write about the patterns behind it all—how place shapes behavior, how people create momentum, and how a next chapter begins.

Virginia Tech Dual degrees — Human Resource Management & Hospitality and Tourism
Real Estate Six-figure production, relocation-focused in St. Petersburg, FL
Sacred Space Studios Founder — creator infrastructure and community in Tampa Bay
Hospitality Background in hospitality operations, experience design, and service strategy
Wellness & Training Yoga teacher training, embodiment practices, and intentional living
Community Building Creator partnerships, mastermind facilitation, and ecosystem development

I believe the best work happens when people have the right environment, the right conversation, and the right people around them. That belief runs through everything I build.

I work with a small number of people and projects at a time so each one receives real attention. If you're building something that needs clarity, connection, or someone who can see the larger pattern, let's talk.
