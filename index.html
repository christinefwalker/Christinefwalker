01 / The thread

A career can hold more than one chapter without becoming a collection of disconnected parts.

I build the conditions that help people and ideas move forward.

I’ve built businesses, coached founders, facilitated agile teams, worked across hospitality and real estate, and designed a life around greater freedom. The common thread is not a title. It is helping people see the next move clearly and create the structure to make it real.

I believe environment shapes behavior, conversation creates clarity, and the right people can change the pace of a project. This site is where those ideas—and the life around them—have room to breathe.
