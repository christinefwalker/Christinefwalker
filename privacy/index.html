The details / Updated October 6, 2026

Privacy, plainly.

This site is here to make connections—not build a dossier. Here’s what is collected and where it goes.

What you tell Christine

The contact form requires your name, email, phone, problem description, and permission to follow up. Business name is optional. Don’t send bank, credit, Social Security, tax, or funding information, account numbers, or sensitive documents. Existing project and download forms remain available and collect the information shown on those forms.

Netlify Forms processes submissions and applies spam filtering. Inquiry records and their follow-up status are stored in Netlify’s managed Postgres database, separately from referral click records. Existing legacy-form archives may also remain in Netlify Blobs. Access to the Connector admin and its data requires an authorized administrator account.

Follow-up and the CRM

Christine uses your information to understand and respond to your inquiry. When the existing GoHighLevel integration is configured, form information is forwarded to that CRM to support follow-up. GoHighLevel API contact records include your name, email, optional phone, and source/segment tags; an enabled workflow webhook may also receive the inquiry details.

Your information is not sold. It may be processed by Netlify and the configured CRM services for the purposes described here. A message to Christine is not automatically submitted to Fugio and is not consent to receive texts from Fugio.

Referral clicks

When you follow the funding connection, this site records a referral click before redirecting you to Fugio. The record includes a random click identifier, timestamp, random session identifier, source, limited campaign labels, landing-page path, problem, button placement, and referral destination. We do not store your IP address, full referrer URL, or contact details in these click records. Netlify may process technical request information to operate and secure the site.

Session storage remembers your landing page and campaign context during this visit. The redirect sets a first-party session cookie for referral attribution. These are used for the connection journey, not advertising profiles. Campaign links should never contain personal information. Christine may earn a commission from affiliate referrals.

Optional analytics

On the Connector pages, first-party page and solution views are recorded only if you choose “Allow analytics.” Your preference is kept in local storage. Declining does not prevent you from contacting Christine or following a referral.

Some preserved legacy pages still use GoHighLevel’s external tracking script, which may process technical visit information and use its own identifiers. Those scripts are not loaded on the new homepage, cash-flow page, contact page, or this privacy page.

After you leave this site

Fugio handles information you submit on its own site under its own privacy policies. The referral URL identifies Christine’s affiliate relationship. When the verified webhook integration is configured, Fugio can send partner referral-status or commission-event notifications. This site stores event identifiers, timestamps, partner/submission references and relevant statuses—not raw payloads, client contact details, or financial documents. We do not claim to match a partner update to an individual referral click without a supported identifier.

Questions and requests

To request access, correction, or deletion of your inquiry information, contact Christine. Information is kept only as needed for follow-up, referral records, and legitimate business obligations; deletion requests are reviewed across the applicable submission and CRM systems.
